Delete a Postcard Trails: Tile Match account

Developer:

The public support route has not been configured. Publication is blocked until the developer supplies the real contact details and processing expectations.

Request deletion in the game

Open Settings → Delete My Account and Data and review the confirmation. The game verifies the completion response before removing its attributable local data. Keep a pending deletion installation's data intact until it has verified completion.

Request deletion by email

No installation, payment, or browser sign-in is required to email a request.

Email an account deletion request

State that the request concerns Postcard Trails: Tile Match account deletion. Include the complete saved deletion recovery code, if available. A player ID helps locate the account, but does not establish ownership.

Verify ownership

  1. A saved TMDEL1 deletion recovery code includes its account locator. Possession authorizes deletion, so keep it private and send it only to the monitored support address.
  2. A surviving authenticated installation can sign a random support challenge through Settings → Deletion Code and Support. Support verifies the signed result.
  3. A linked player can voluntarily recover through the original Google Play Games profile in the game. Reinstallation is not required to contact support.

Names, email addresses, screenshots, device descriptions, player IDs, and claimed purchases alone do not prove ownership. Without usable proof, support cannot authorize destructive deletion. A guest who lost cached credentials can use a saved valid deletion code to authorize deletion, but the code cannot recover the account or its progress.

What deletion removes

Deletion covers the Unity Authentication account and Cloud Save gameplay in both development and production: campaign progress, the local wallet backup, power-ups, reward and purchase caches, battle pass claims and timestamps, wheel records, player files, and deletion recovery proof. Connected installations remove their attributable local copies after verifying completion. Other devices cannot be erased while offline.

Temporary deletion processing records, completion receipts, and empty blocking records remain only to finish interrupted requests and prevent old requests from recreating data. Empty blocking records remain for at least 65 minutes after account deletion. Background processing runs hourly; removal of these temporary records becomes eligible after that safety period and proceeds once related requests are complete. Backlogs, service interruptions, and unresolved requests can delay cleanup beyond 24 hours. Gameplay is not retained in those records. Unity and Google may separately retain operational logs under their applicable policies; this flow does not claim to erase provider logs or refund purchases.

Analytics history

Full account deletion also submits Analytics history erasure requests to Unity in both service environments using the account identifier. A completion receipt confirms acceptance of these requests, together with verified Authentication and Cloud Save removal; it does not confirm that Unity has finished erasing Analytics history. Unity processes Analytics erasure asynchronously.

Analytics stays disabled for the deleting app session, including after Continue as Guest. Local Analytics history is removed on the next app launch, or by uninstalling. Turning analytics off in Settings stops new recording on that device and does not itself erase historical observations. Previously recorded observations may still be delivered.

Disconnected installations cannot immediately receive another device's deletion or consent choice. On reconnect, late Analytics data from another installation may require a further authorized erasure request through support. The installation that initiated deletion must not replay its own deleted history.

Processing expectations

If ownership cannot be verified or a service operation is unresolved, support explains the limitation. A completion receipt can resolve an interrupted in-game deletion after account removal is verified and both Analytics erasure submissions are accepted.
