Privacy policy

ShadowZz develops Postcard Trails: Tile Match and is responsible for the game's data handling described in this policy.

Privacy contact: postcardtrails.tilematch@gmail.com. ShadowZz aims to reply within one day. Completing a request can take longer because ownership must be verified and the relevant services must process it.

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Optional gameplay analytics requires a separate choice in the game. Declining analytics does not block campaign play, rewards, or account recovery.

Information the game handles

  • Account information. Unity Authentication assigns a game account identifier and manages sign-in credentials. When an existing Google Play Games profile is connected, profile identifiers link that profile to the game account. The Google Play Games display name can be shown in the account interface. The game does not receive the Google account password.
  • Saved progress. The device stores campaign progress, coins, power-ups, reward and battle-pass state, and other gameplay progress. Unity Cloud Save stores account-associated backups so progress can be backed up and recovered. Account linking, save comparison, and recovery also use identifiers and information needed to distinguish the correct account and save.
  • Online operations. Unity services process the account identifier and requests needed for cloud backups, configuration, server time, wheel eligibility, account recovery, and deletion. Those services also receive technical information needed to handle requests, including IP addresses and service logs.
  • Device information and preferences. The game stores installation identifiers, authentication state, device preferences, privacy choices, and information needed to safely finish interrupted save or deletion operations. Analytics choices are specific to the account and device; recovering a cloud save does not import another device's analytics permission.
  • Optional analytics. With permission, Unity Analytics receives session activity; campaign starts, wins, failures, and exits; level and attempt information; elapsed play time; power-up and continue counts; coin additions and spending; feature interactions; and account-connection and recovery outcomes. Analytics also processes the game account and installation identifiers, timestamps, device and software information, IP address, approximate location derived from the network, and connectivity information. The account identifier allows consenting observations from recovered devices to be associated with the same account. Custom gameplay fields do not include email addresses, Google Play Games profile identifiers or display names, passwords, payment details, or support secrets.
  • Engine diagnostics. Unity engine diagnostics is separate from the optional gameplay-analytics choice. When enabled, it processes technical device, software, and performance information for engine reliability. Unity's player privacy information explains its processing.
  • Support correspondence. Emailing support provides the sender's email address, message, attachments, and any account or ownership information deliberately included. A valid deletion recovery code or signed support response may be needed to verify an account-deletion request.

The game does not request access to contacts, the microphone, camera, or precise device location. An IP address can still reveal an approximate location to an online service.

Why information is used

Account and gameplay information provides the game, backs up and recovers progress, prevents incorrect account or reward changes, and fulfils deletion requests. Technical information supports service operation, security, reliability, and troubleshooting. Optional analytics helps ShadowZz understand progression, difficulty, the game economy, and feature use. Support information is used to answer the request, verify ownership when necessary, and address privacy obligations.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, providing requested game and account services relies on performing the service relationship; optional gameplay analytics relies on consent; security and support rely on legitimate interests where permitted; and fulfilling applicable legal obligations relies on those obligations.

Analytics choices

Use Settings → Analytics Privacy to allow analytics or withdraw permission. Analytics is not recorded while the choice is unknown or denied, or when the account or privacy configuration cannot safely support collection. Declining does not cause earlier gameplay to be collected later. Each installation has its own choice.

Withdrawing permission stops new gameplay-analytics recording on that device. Previously recorded observations may still be delivered, and withdrawal does not itself erase existing history. Full account deletion requests historical erasure. For other privacy requests about analytics, contact the support email above.

Online account and cloud services, support email, and engine diagnostics have separate purposes and are not all disabled by the optional gameplay-analytics choice. Campaign play can continue offline; online features need connectivity.

  • Google: Google Play Games profile services, Google Play distribution, and Gmail for support correspondence. Read Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: hosting these public information pages through GitHub Pages. Website requests reach GitHub, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. Read GitHub's Privacy Statement.

ShadowZz uses these services for the purposes described here. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect rights, investigate abuse, or secure the service. Providers can separately process certain operational information under their own applicable terms and privacy notices.

This policy describes the current Android integration, which does not include a live advertising SDK or real-money purchasing provider. New advertising or purchasing services require corresponding privacy disclosures and controls before use.

Retention

Cloud gameplay backups and account records are retained until the account is deleted. Local saves and settings remain on the installation until removed through the applicable game controls, app-data removal, or uninstalling. Removing an installation alone does not request deletion of its online account or cloud backup.

Unity's Analytics guidance describes raw-event retention of 13 months, with metric and profile data retained until deletion. Analytics erasure is asynchronous after a request is accepted. A completion receipt from the game confirms the accepted erasure request, not that Unity has completed its processing.

Temporary deletion-processing records, receipts, and empty records that prevent old requests from recreating data are retained to finish and secure deletion. Empty blocking records remain for at least 65 minutes after account deletion. Background cleanup runs hourly and becomes eligible after the safety period and related requests complete. Backlogs, service interruptions, or unresolved operations can extend cleanup beyond 24 hours.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve the request, verify and document its handling, and meet applicable legal obligations. Unity, Google, and GitHub may retain separate security, operational, or legally required records under their applicable privacy notices. The game account-deletion process does not promise removal of every provider log.

Account deletion

Request deletion using Settings → Delete My Account and Data, or follow the public account-deletion instructions to contact support without installing the game or signing in on this website. Ownership must be verified before destructive account changes are authorised.

Deletion covers the game Authentication account and attributable Cloud Save data in both service environments, and submits Analytics erasure requests in both environments. The deleting installation disables Analytics for the rest of that app session; local Analytics history is removed on the next app launch or by uninstalling. Offline installations cannot be erased remotely. An installation that reconnects after deletion can require additional handling, and late Analytics data may require a further verified erasure request.

Deleting the game account does not delete the Google account or Google Play Games profile and does not refund purchases. The deletion page explains the verification options, retained processing records, and completion limitations.

Privacy requests and rights

Depending on applicable law, rights can include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, and a complaint to the relevant data-protection authority. Contact postcardtrails.tilematch@gmail.com to make a request or appeal a response. State that the request concerns Postcard Trails: Tile Match. Only provide information necessary for the request. ShadowZz may need to verify the requester's identity or account authority.

Do not email Google passwords, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive documents. Keep any account-deletion recovery code private and share it only through the monitored support route when needed for deletion.

Security and international processing

The game uses protected local storage, account-based access controls, and encrypted network connections for its online services. No storage or transmission method eliminates every security risk.

Unity, Google, and GitHub operate internationally. Information can be processed outside the player's country. Their privacy notices and relevant data-processing terms describe processing locations and transfer safeguards. Contact ShadowZz for questions about the game's use of these services.

Adult audience

Postcard Trails: Tile Match is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. This audience statement does not mean that the game verifies every player's age. If information is believed to have been collected from a child, contact support so the circumstances and applicable deletion requirements can be addressed.
